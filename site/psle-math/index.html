Are these real PSLE questions?

No. Every question is original and uses the problem-solving heuristics taught in Singapore primary schools. We never use real or copied exam papers.

Who is it for?

P6 students preparing for PSLE Math, and P5 students who are starting on word-problem heuristics.

How should my child use it?

One method a day for about four weeks: read the worked example together, cover the solution, let your child redo it alone, then try the practice question.
