# Math Island: free daily PSLE problem-sum game

Math Island is a free browser game by Edge 4 Exams Singapore that trains PSLE Math problem sums in 15 minutes a day. Your child builds the bar model, finds 1 unit, then answers. Every correct, quick answer earns shells, and shells hatch island pals.

Play: https://edge4exams.com/math-island/ (free, no sign-up, works on phones, tablets and computers)

## World 1: Bar Bridge

- Level 1: "times as many" questions (with a total or a difference)
- Level 2: "more than" questions
- Level 3: three-person questions
- Each level needs 6 questions solved, 4 of them on the first try with no hints
- Then a 5-question gate exam with no hints and no model help; pass with 4 of 5. Missing the gate means 2 more practice questions before a fresh attempt.

More worlds (Supposition Farm, Before–After Tower, Rewind Cave) are coming.

## How a daily session works

- 10 questions, about 15 minutes. Finishing keeps the day streak going.
- Shells: 10 for a correct answer, up to 8 for speed, 4 for a first try with no hints, plus a combo bonus.
- 120 shells hatch an egg, at most one a day. Shells are only earned by solving; nothing can be bought.
- Egg odds: Common 60%, Rare 28%, Epic 9%, Legendary 2.5%, Mythic 0.5%. A Legendary or better is guaranteed by the 30th egg.
- At the end your child gets a share card (like Wordle) and passes the phone back. The parent report shows each question, the time taken, where mistakes happened, possible guesses, and a question to ask your child.

## Privacy

Progress and reports stay on the device in browser storage. Nothing is uploaded.
