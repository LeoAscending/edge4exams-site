Math Island

A free game that trains PSLE Math problem sums in 15 minutes a day. Your child builds the bar model, finds 1 unit, then answers. Every correct, quick answer earns shells, and shells hatch island pals.

World 1 is Bar Bridge: "times as many", "more than" and three-person questions. Pass the gate exam to show mastery. A short report for parents comes after every session.
